📺 Bright TV Viewership Analysis Project

📌 Project Overview

This project analyses Bright TV viewership data to uncover meaningful audience trends and translate them into practical business recommendations. The goal was to move beyond simply reporting viewership numbers and answer the questions that matter most to decision-makers:

* What content attracts the largest audiences?
* Which genres perform best?
* When are viewers most likely to watch?
* Which programmes are underperforming?
* How does viewership change over time?
* What actions can Bright TV take to increase and retain viewers?

Using data cleaning, exploratory analysis, pivot tables, and data visualisation, the project transforms raw viewership data into clear insights that can support programming, scheduling, and growth decisions.

---

## Business Problem

Bright TV operates in a competitive and rapidly changing South African media environment. Traditional television continues to reach a large audience, but viewer attention is increasingly fragmented across streaming services, social media and other digital platforms.

The key challenge was to understand Bright TV's existing viewership performance and identify opportunities to grow the audience.

The analysis was designed to provide the CEO and other decision-makers with a clear view of:

* High-performing and low-performing programmes
* Popular content genres
* Peak viewing periods
* Daily and monthly viewership patterns
* Potential opportunities to improve programming and scheduling
* Strategic recommendations for increasing overall viewership

---

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project were to:

1. Clean and prepare the raw dataset for analysis.

3. Build pivot tables to answer key business questions.

## Key Insights

The analysis focuses on identifying the relationship between **content, scheduling, and audience behaviour**.

The most important areas of investigation include:

### High-Performing Content

The project identifies the programmes that generate the strongest viewership. These programmes represent an opportunity for Bright TV to:

* Protect successful time slots
* Develop similar content
* Create spin-offs or related programming
* Use strong shows to introduce newer programmes

### Genre Performance

Analysing genres helps Bright TV understand what type of content audiences are most interested in.

High-performing genres can support:

* Future content investment
* Programming decisions
* Content acquisition
* Audience targeting

### Peak Viewing Periods

Understanding when audiences are watching is essential for effective scheduling.

The analysis identifies opportunities to:

* Place premium content during peak viewing periods
* Use lower-cost content during low-viewership periods
* Promote new programmes around successful programmes
* Improve advertising opportunities

### Underperforming Programmes

Low viewership does not automatically mean that a programme should be cancelled.

The analysis provides a starting point for investigating whether performance is affected by:

* Time slot
* Genre
* Competition
* Marketing
* Audience awareness

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis and broader trends in the South African viewing market, Bright TV can increase viewership by focusing on the following areas.

### 1. Use Data to Drive Programming Decisions

Bright TV should regularly monitor programme performance and use audience data to guide:

* Content investment
* Scheduling
* Programme renewals
* Marketing decisions

### 2. Strengthen High-Performing Content

Successful programmes should be treated as strategic assets.

Bright TV can use them to:

* Attract viewers to new programmes
* Build themed programming blocks
* Develop related content
* Increase advertising value

### 3. Optimise the Programming Schedule

The strongest programmes should be scheduled during the periods with the highest audience activity.

A useful strategy is:

**Strong Programme → New Programme → Strong Programme**

This can help transfer viewers from established programmes to newer content.

### 4. Invest in Local and Relevant Content

South African audiences can be attracted through content that is:

* Relatable
* Culturally relevant
* Locally produced
* Representative of diverse communities and stories

### 5. Expand Beyond Traditional Television

Viewership is increasingly spread across television, streaming platforms, and social media.

Bright TV should consider a multi-platform approach by:

* Creating short-form content for social media
* Promoting programmes online
* Making selected content available on demand
* Using digital platforms to attract younger audiences

### 6. Build a Continuous Measurement Culture

Rather than analysing performance only occasionally, Bright TV should establish regular reporting and dashboard monitoring.

Key metrics should include:

* Total viewership
* Average viewership
* Programme performance
* Genre performance
* Peak viewing periods
* Viewership trends

## Recommended Dashboard

An executive dashboard for Bright TV should include:

1. **Top 10 Programmes by Viewership**
2. **Viewership by Genre**
3. **Viewership by Time Slot**
